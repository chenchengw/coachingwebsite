About

I've been where you are.

A decade leading engineering at LinkedIn and Instacart. Today: AI advisor + builder, executive coach, writer — by design, not by accident.

The Path

A decade of becoming a different kind of leader

15 years of experience from LinkedIn to Instacart — IC to Engineering Director leading 50+ engineers. A senior leader's perspective on how performance, promotion, and influence actually work. Each transition required becoming a different kind of leader:

  • IC to Tech Lead — it stopped being about my code
  • Tech Lead to Manager — the scoreboard changed and nobody told me
  • Manager to Senior Manager — my job became replacing myself
  • Senior Manager to Director — politics weren't optional anymore
  • Director to my own shape — I walked away from the title to build a resilient career

What carried me through each rewrite: seeing what I was already great at, doubling down on what was worth building, letting go of what no longer fit. I help clients do the same — that's the shape of the work.

My Approach

How I help you build your resilient career

The old career compounding model — build deep, climb the ladder, depth wins — is being broken by AI.

Execution is cheap. Title doesn't brand you anymore. What carries you instead is structurally different: four moves you build yourself, because company and title no longer do it for you. Each makes the others stronger — your MOAT.

The coaching work: who you want to become, and who you are now that title doesn't speak for you.

  • Make — Show your output when talk is the bottleneck — writing, github, products. Your work speaks for you.
  • Outlook — See what's coming, invest before it's obvious. Invest ahead of the rubric, not after it changes.
  • Audience — When your title doesn't speak for you, personal branding does. Your name in channels you own, not borrowed from any platform.
  • Tribe — Peers and support, on purpose. The foundation for resilience and a purposeful life.

More than a career, it's a life that adds up to purpose.
