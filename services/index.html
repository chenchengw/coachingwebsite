Three ways to build a resilient career

A deep partnership — 1:1 coaching, unlimited AICoach support between sessions, and technical consulting when it applies. Three engagement shapes, for the moment you're actually in.

In the Interview Loop 5 sessions · 1:1

Tell the story of what you actually did.

"I've done the work. I just can't tell it in a way that lands — and the next loop is in two weeks."

Your experience isn't the problem. The account of it is. We rebuild your core stories so what you navigated carries its real weight — and so you speak with the presence of someone already at the next level. Built on a decade of hiring and calibration at LinkedIn and Instacart; clients have landed offers at companies like OpenAI and Roblox. Walk away with stories that are true, specific, and yours.
