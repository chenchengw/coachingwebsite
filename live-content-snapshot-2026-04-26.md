## Services — *How I work with clients*

**Intro:** *All sessions are 1:1. I start with a free strategy session so you can decide if the fit and chemistry feel right.*

### Card 1 — Something Has to Change

**Tagline:** *The path you planned for looks nothing like it did a year ago. You're not sure if you need a new role, a new company, or a new chapter entirely — you just know something has to give.*

**We work on:** separating burnout from misalignment, understanding what you actually want (not what you think you should want), and making the next move with conviction instead of panic.

### Card 2 — The Rules Keep Changing

**Tagline:** *You're adapting as fast as you can — new skills, new expectations, new scoreboard. But the feedback doesn't match the effort, and nobody's telling you how you're being evaluated.*

**We work on:** reading the real scoreboard, understanding what your level actually demands, and building a leadership style that's yours — not a performance.

### Card 3 — The Room Got Quieter

**Tagline:** *The higher you've climbed, the lonelier it gets. You're navigating politics, managing up, and making high-stakes calls with less and less honest input.*

**We work on:** thinking partnership, navigating power dynamics without losing yourself, and making decisions with clear eyes when nobody will tell you the truth.

## About — *I've been where you are.*

[circular photo of Amy]

Drawing from 10+ years at LinkedIn and Instacart — where I grew from IC to Engineering Director leading 50+ engineers — I bring an insider understanding of how performance, promotion, and influence actually work inside tech organizations. Each transition required becoming a different kind of leader:

- **IC to Tech Lead** — it stopped being about my code
- **Tech Lead to Manager** — the scoreboard changed and nobody told me
- **Manager to Senior Manager** — my job became replacing myself
- **Senior Manager to Director** — politics weren't optional anymore
- **Director to Builder** — I walked away from the title and started building again

I navigated all of them as often the only Asian and woman in the room — which meant less room to figure it out in public.

I coach from pattern recognition, not frameworks or clichés. I help clients clarify what's really being asked, spot the patterns that are hard to see from the inside, and move forward with clarity and intention.

## Notable observations vs. the older `homepage.md` spec

The live site has evolved significantly past `CoachWebsite/homepage.md`:

1. **Hero is fully different.** Spec has *"You need to be more assertive..."* opener; live has *"I Help Tech Professionals Navigate Inflection Points"* — non-gendered, pain-recognition framing. Live is much sharper.
2. **Tagline gone.** Spec has *"Leadership coaching for women in tech — grounded in real experience..."*; live drops the tagline entirely and lets the hero do the positioning work.
3. **Bridge section is new.** 4 pain-recognition statements between hero and services that aren't in the spec at all.
4. **Services are pain-anchored, not outcome-themed.** Spec has Clarity / Confidence / Navigation; live has *Something Has to Change / The Rules Keep Changing / The Room Got Quieter* — pattern-recognition framing that mirrors the marketing-expert input on pain-first positioning.
5. **5-level transition map extended to 6.** Spec ends at *Director to Executive*; live has *Director to Builder* — Amy's actual current move.
6. **Testimonials are tighter.** Each entry is shorter than the spec versions; some cuts feel intentional (Benji's "I highly recommend" dropped).
7. **Contact form mirrors services pillars.** The dropdown options match the three card titles, creating a cascade from services display to contact intent.
8. **Final CTA has a 3-step timeline.** Clarity Call → Design the Fit → The Work. Not in the spec.
9. **Newsletter integration is more prominent.** Modal + iframe + footer + nav link + final-CTA box. Substack lift is built in throughout.

**Implication for any strategy work:** the live site is the canonical reference, not the spec. Strategy recommendations should compare against this snapshot, not against `homepage.md`.
